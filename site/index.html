How to read this

Direct contribution
A check from AIPAC PAC (FEC committee C00797670) to the candidate's campaign. These are capped at $5,000 per election.
Earmarked (bundled)
Donations from individuals that AIPAC PAC collected and forwarded to the candidate. This is usually the largest share of AIPAC-linked money.
Super PAC spending
Ads and other spending by United Democracy Project (C00799031), AIPAC's super PAC. This money is spent for or against a candidate. It is not given to the candidate, so it is shown separately and does not count toward the AIPAC PAC total.

Figures cover the 2022, 2024 and 2026 election cycles, since AIPAC PAC began in late 2021. "No AIPAC PAC money found" means none appears in FEC filings processed as of the date shown. Recent money may not be reported yet. A candidate with no FEC registration raised too little to have to file. Only candidates printed on the ballot are shown, not write-ins.
